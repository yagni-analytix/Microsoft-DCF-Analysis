# Methodology

**Prepared by:** Yagni Patel

## Investment question

Can Microsoft's AI and cloud investments generate sufficient long-term free cash flow growth and returns to justify the market valuation?

## Historical treatment

- Consolidated historical analysis covers FY2021A-FY2026A.
- Primary segment analysis uses FY2023A-FY2026A on Microsoft's FY2025 recast basis.
- FY2021A-FY2022A legacy segment data is presented separately and excluded from cross-basis growth analysis.

## Forecast construction

The model forecasts each reportable segment independently.

### Productivity & Business Processes

Drivers include Microsoft 365 seats and ARPU, Copilot monetization, LinkedIn, Dynamics 365, subscription mix, and AI serving costs.

### Intelligent Cloud

Drivers include Azure consumption, AI services, cloud migration, capacity availability, datacenter utilization, depreciation, energy costs, and infrastructure efficiency.

### More Personal Computing

Drivers include Windows OEM, search advertising, gaming and content, devices, and PC and console cyclicality.

Segment revenue and operating income consolidate into the company forecast.

## Cash flow

The model distinguishes:

- **Cash-Based UFCF:** NOPAT + D&A and other noncash charges - cash CapEx - change in operating NWC.
- **Lease-Adjusted UFCF:** NOPAT + D&A and other noncash charges - total CapEx - change in operating NWC.

Total CapEx includes cash CapEx and finance-lease additions. The DCF uses Lease-Adjusted UFCF.

## ROIC

Invested capital is calculated from stockholders' equity plus interest-bearing debt and finance-lease liabilities, less cash, short-term investments, and equity and other investments.

ROIC uses average beginning/ending invested capital. Incremental ROIC uses change in NOPAT divided by change in ending invested capital.

## DCF

- Explicit forecast: FY2027E-FY2036E
- Mid-year convention
- WACC: 9.47%
- Terminal growth: 3.00%
- Enterprise-value-to-equity-value bridge includes cash, investments, debt, and finance-lease liabilities
- FY2026 diluted weighted-average shares are used as a share-count proxy

## Reverse DCF

The reverse DCF solves for a percentage-point uplift to consolidated revenue growth. The uplift is largest in FY2027E and fades linearly to zero by FY2036E. Operating margins, tax rates, reinvestment, WACC, and terminal growth remain fixed at base-case levels.

The solved uplift is 7.41 percentage points, producing market-implied FY2027E revenue growth of 23.62%.

## Buffett-inspired five-tenet framework

The framework is analyst-created and is not an official Berkshire Hathaway checklist. It weights:

| Tenet | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Business clarity and predictability | 15% |
| Durable competitive advantage | 25% |
| Financial quality and earnings power | 20% |
| Capital allocation and incremental returns | 20% |
| Valuation and margin of safety | 20% |

Each tenet receives a score from 1 to 5. Weighted points are calculated as:

```text
Weight x Score / 5 x 100
```

The overall score is 74.5 / 100. Excluding valuation, the business-quality score is 88.1 / 100. The framework deliberately allows an exceptional company to receive a cautious investment stance when the market price provides no margin of safety.

## Margin-of-safety references

- Base intrinsic value: $367.85
- 10% margin-of-safety price: $331.06
- 15% margin-of-safety price: $312.67
- 20% margin-of-safety price: $294.28

## Final stance

The model uses a **WATCH / WAIT** classification rather than a buy/sell label. Reassessment is warranted if the price approaches the margin-of-safety range or if reported AI monetization, Intelligent Cloud margins, CapEx normalization, and incremental ROIC materially outperform the base assumptions.
